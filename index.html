How it works

A straightforward monthly routine

Start with a few details, then update your records as you go.

01
Add your income
Enter your monthly income so you can compare spending with what came in.
Income can vary. Update the amount to reflect client payments, salary, or other earnings for the month.
02
Record your expenses
Add an amount and a note, then organize the purchase under a category. Voice input is available when it is more convenient.
Keep it simple. A short description is enough to make an entry easier to recognize later.
03
Review your month
Use category totals, budgets, and recurring costs to understand where your money went and decide what to adjust.
Review regularly. Checking in during the month makes it easier to keep budgets up to date.
Privacy & Security

Your records stay in your browser

Cashero stores your financial records locally in the browser rather than syncing them to an account.

Stored on your device
Expenses, budgets, and goals are saved in your browser. They are not available on other devices unless you export and move your records yourself.
Optional app lock
Set a PIN to add a lock when opening the app. Keep your recovery phrase somewhere safe in case you need to regain access.
Export your records
Export your history as a CSV file so you can keep a separate copy or use it in another spreadsheet.
